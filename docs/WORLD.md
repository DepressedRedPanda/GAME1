# Heavy Mettle — world and continuity guide

**Canonical:** Space western, grounded sci-fi frontier. An amnesiac, human-looking android lives among miners, couriers, settlers, debtors, and outlaws at Cinder's Rest. Nearby Old Relay is industrial infrastructure in the badlands. Story comes from survival, debt, loyalty, scarce power and water, and the protagonist's missing history. **No spells, fantasy magic or imported Hollowreach fantasy campaign.**

**Technology:** Machines need actual power; battery cells are both an energy source and a bargaining chip. Decaying or ancient industrial machinery can hold traces of the protagonist's origins. The label **"Hollow technology" is PROPOSED terminology** from the user's prompt and is not established canon until accepted; if adopted it refers to technology, not fantasy magic.

**Established characters:**
- Oren Bale: freight clerk, knows the harsh economics of supply control.
- Nima Voss: medic who treats the android as a person.
- Tessa Rhun: tavern keeper, sells silence and hears everything.
- Silt Jack: outlaw leader, protects displaced miners.

**First playable quest:** Missing Cells. Oren's missing power cells are sustaining a water pump at Old Relay. Fight or negotiate, decide where the cells go, then observe a local consequence. See docs/QUESTS.md and docs/UNIFIED_GAME_ARCHITECTURE.md.

**Story constraints:** Small consequences over galaxy-scale reputation; dry humor and practical moral tradeoffs. Mystery revealed in fragments, not lore dumps. Keep proposed quests and narrative additions outside canon until user approval.
